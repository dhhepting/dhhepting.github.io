Chaos game: the dragon curve, seen from each map's own centre

Each point is the previous point multiplied by one of two 3×3 matrices, chosen at random. Each matrix is built from its centre: step into the centre, scale and rotate, step back. Drag a centre (on the canvas or with the sliders) and watch the attractor follow.
