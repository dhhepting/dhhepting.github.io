From three vertices to every pixel

The vertex shader runs once per vertex. The rasterizer then works out which pixels the triangle covers and blends each vertex's outputs for them. The fragment shader runs once for every one of those pixels. Drag the vertices, shrink the image, and hover a pixel.

This browser does not support WebGL2.
